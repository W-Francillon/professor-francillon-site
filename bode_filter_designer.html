2. Key Bode Plot Rules for Laboratory Verification

  1. The -3\text{ dB} Half-Power Cutoff Point (f = f_c): At the cutoff frequency f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}, the capacitive reactance equals the resistance (X_C = R). Consequently, the output voltage drops to \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707 of the passband voltage (20\log_{10}(0.707) = -3.01\text{ dB}), and a 1st-order low-pass filter introduces an exact -45^\circ phase lag.
  2. Decade Roll-Off Slopes: Each reactive energy-storage element (C or L) contributes -20\text{ dB/decade} (-6\text{ dB/octave}) of attenuation beyond f_c and up to 90^\circ of asymptotic phase shift. Cascading two poles in the Sallen-Key Low-Pass topology doubles the stopband roll-off to -40\text{ dB/decade} and sweeps the phase from 0^\circ to -180^\circ.
  3. Resonant Peaking in 2nd-Order Filters (Q > 0.707): In the Sallen-Key topology, increasing the non-inverting stage gain K = 1 + \frac{R_f}{R_g} raises the quality factor Q = \frac{1}{3 - K}. At K = 1.59 (Q = 0.707), the response is maximally flat (Butterworth); above K = 1.59, a resonant gain peak emerges right near f_c before oscillating at K = 3.
