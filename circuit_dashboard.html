Tile A • Ohm’s Law & DC Power

Ohm’s Law:
V = I \cdot R \quad \big| \quad I = \dfrac{V}{R} \quad \big| \quad R = \dfrac{V}{I}
Watt’s Power Law:
P = V \cdot I = I^2 R = \dfrac{V^2}{R}

RLC Complex Impedance (Z) & Phasor Solver

Tile F • Network Equivalency & Kirchhoff’s Laws

Series / Parallel R, L, C Solver & KVL / KCL Reference

Compute equivalent 2- or 3-branch combinations (leave 3rd blank for 2-branch) and review Kirchhoff’s Laws.

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL): \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{N} V_k = 0 around any closed loop (Conservation of Energy).
Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL): \displaystyle\sum I_{\text{in}} = \sum I_{\text{out}} at any node (Conservation of Charge).
